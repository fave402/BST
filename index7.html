About the Founder

Born and raised in Lagos, Nigeria, Idris Seun studied Aeronautical Engineering at Imperial College London. After a decade of working with global airlines, he envisioned an airline that made air travel more accessible to everyone.

In 2015, he founded Peiwa Airlines to offer reliable, affordable flights with a touch of excellence. His goal is to bridge continents and bring people closer through seamless travel experiences.
