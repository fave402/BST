About the Founder

Founded by the remarkable Peiwa Team of the Upskill 2025 cohort 1 of Genesys Tech Hub , Peiwa Airlines is the brainchild of visionary leaders dedicated to revolutionizing air travel. With a passion for connecting people and cultures, our founder envisioned an airline that prioritizes comfort, reliability, and exceptional service. Under their guidance, Peiwa Airlines has grown into a trusted name in the aviation industry, known for its commitment to safety, innovation, and customer satisfaction. Their leadership continues to inspire our team to reach new heights and deliver unparalleled travel experiences to passengers worldwide.
